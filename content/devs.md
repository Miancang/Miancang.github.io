## Development Projects

### SmartFill AI

**AI-powered smart form filler extension that auto-fills web forms, mainly designed for Math PhD application**

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Miancang/SmartFill_AI)

SmartFill AI is an intelligent browser extension developed with AI assistance that automatically fills web forms. The extension is primarily designed to streamline the Math PhD application process by intelligently populating application forms with relevant information.

**Key Features:**
- AI-powered form detection and field analysis
- Smart auto-fill for common application fields
- Customizable templates for different application forms
- Designed specifically for academic applications

**Development:**
- Developed with AI assistance
- Focus on user experience and automation efficiency
